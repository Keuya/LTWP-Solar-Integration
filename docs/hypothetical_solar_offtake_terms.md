# Hypothetical solar offtake term sheet

> **Case-study assumption only.** This is a fictional, non-binding term sheet created to complete a modelling exercise. It was not issued by LTWP, Kenya Power (KPLC), a lender or government body; no negotiations or approvals are implied. Do not send it as an offer or describe it as an actual LTWP transaction.

## Transaction outline

| Item | Illustrative case-study term |
|---|---|
| Seller | Hypothetical LTWP Solar ProjectCo, a ring-fenced SPV; no actual LTWP affiliate or approval is implied. |
| Buyer | Hypothetical licensed utility offtaker, modelled as KPLC solely for the teaching case; requires separate confirmation, consent and legal review. |
| Project | 77.5 MWp DC / 74.62 MWac solar PV colocated near the existing LTWP facility. |
| Contracted product | Net solar energy metered at the approved point of connection, after station use and agreed electrical losses. |
| Term | 20 years from commercial operation date. |
| Target COD | 24 months after financial close, subject to permits, connection works and conditions precedent. |
| Tariff | USD 65/MWh, flat nominal, no escalation. An analyst assumption, not a Kenyan benchmark or quoted tariff. |
| Settlement currency | Invoice stated in USD; paid in KES at an agreed published spot-rate convention on invoice date. FX conversion and convertibility risk require negotiation. |
| Billing and payment | Monthly invoice; payment within 30 calendar days of receipt of a valid invoice. Late interest at an agreed reference rate plus margin. |
| Metering | Revenue meter at the approved point of connection, with calibrated check meter, data access and dispute procedure. |

## Dispatch, curtailment and grid allocation

- Existing wind dispatch and contractual rights rank ahead of the new solar case. Solar uses residual export headroom at the shared point of connection. The model uses this wind-priority rule for its base case.
- The assumed 310 MW cap is a sensitivity only. The executed connection agreement and system-operator studies must set actual operating limits, ramp constraints and outage treatment.
- System-operator or network curtailment is uncompensated in the base case unless the final PPA, connection agreement and law establish deemed-energy compensation.
- Buyer-caused curtailment after the plant is available and the grid is available is modelled as deemed energy, capped by contracted energy and subject to agreed exclusions and evidence.
- SolarCo bears losses, station use and its allocated connection/shared-facility charges up to the meter. No free use of existing infrastructure is assumed.

## Payment security and credit support

- Buyer provides a revolving letter of credit equal to two months of forecast invoices, replenished after drawings.
- The security provider, acceptable form, expiry, draw conditions and renewal period are conditions to financial close.
- Direct agreement, lender cure rights, payment default, prolonged non-payment and termination compensation require negotiation. No guarantee or sovereign support is assumed.

## Conditions precedent and risk allocation

Financial close requires a bankable connection agreement and approved export limit; permits and licences; land and access rights; agreed shared-facility works and cost allocation; approved metering and dispatch protocol; PPA approvals/consents; lender direct agreement; and an agreed construction, testing and COD regime.

Seller bears construction, resource, availability and operating performance risk subject to agreed force majeure and buyer/grid relief. Buyer bears payment obligations for metered energy and any agreed buyer-caused deemed energy. Change-in-law, political force majeure, convertibility, dispute resolution and termination amounts remain open for Kenyan counsel and counterparties.

## Finance-model treatment

Use USD 65/MWh, 20 years, flat nominal tariff, monthly billing and the payment-security assumption only in the clearly labelled illustrative case. Apply the hourly model's exported solar MWh, not available PV generation. Build taxes, FX, working capital, O&M, CAPEX, degradation, curtailment and debt terms separately. Do not call the resulting gross revenue or returns bankable.
